Selling and renting out property in Singapore: common questions

Short answers to the questions owners ask most before they sell or rent out a home. Each answer comes from a longer guide that cites the official HDB, CPF Board, IRAS, URA or CEA page it relies on.

Selling an HDB flat

Can I sell my HDB flat without an agent?

Yes. HDB says you may manage the sale on your own or engage a salesperson for a fee. Either way, you must register the Intent to Sell personally. Selling on your own means you handle pricing, viewings, the OTP and your portion of the resale application.

How much does it cost to sell an HDB flat?

HDB charges each side a non-refundable resale application fee of $80 for a 3-room or bigger flat, or $40 for a 1- or 2-room flat, including GST. Add legal fees, property tax to the end of the year, service and conservancy charges to completion and any agent commission you agree. Commission is not fixed in Singapore.

Do I have to return CPF when I sell my HDB flat?

Yes. The CPF savings used for the flat, with accrued interest, are refunded to your own CPF account, together with any housing grants and their interest. CPF Board says you do not need to top up a shortfall in cash if the flat is sold at market value. CPF Board notes that any option money you received in cash is part of the selling price and must still be refunded to your CPF account before completion.

When do I receive the money from selling my HDB flat?

At resale completion. HDB says cash proceeds are credited to your bank account by the next working day after completion, and it arranges the CPF refund within 7–14 working days of the appointment. If you appoint a private solicitor, the solicitor arranges the CPF refund.

MOP and your next home

When does the 5-year MOP start?

For a new flat, MOP generally starts at key collection; for a resale flat, at resale completion. Periods of non-occupation can be excluded. The applicable period depends on the flat and purchase conditions, so confirm HDB's recorded eligibility date rather than adding five years yourself.

How much CPF do I need to refund when I sell my HDB flat?

Use the required housing refund shown for each owner in CPF's Home ownership dashboard, including principal and accrued interest. Refunds remain CPF savings, not cash sale proceeds. For owners aged 55 and above, refunds first top up the Retirement Account to the applicable Full Retirement Sum.

Can I buy a condo before selling my HDB flat?

Check HDB ownership conditions, financing and ABSD before committing. Selling within six months does not automatically qualify you for an ABSD refund. The married-couple remission has separate citizenship, joint-ownership, sale-deadline and application conditions; the deadline differs for completed and uncompleted purchases.

How long does the HDB resale process take after the OTP?

Allow for the buyer's 21-calendar-day option period, the agreed application deadline, HDB's acceptance checks and about eight weeks from acceptance to completion. The buyer and seller must submit their portions within seven calendar days of each other. Marketing and any approved stay extension are separate.

Do I have to wait 30 months after selling my HDB flat?

Selling an HDB flat after MOP does not by itself trigger a blanket 30-month wait. Private-property disposal, subsidy history, resale levy and the next purchase route have separate rules. Obtain an HFE assessment for the next HDB purchase rather than assuming your present sale makes you eligible.

How long a sale takes

Does selling an HDB flat take only eight weeks?

No. HDB describes completion as about eight weeks after it accepts the resale application. Finding a buyer, the OTP stage and submitting a complete application happen before that clock starts.

How much is the HDB option fee?

The Option Fee is negotiated from S$1 to S$1,000. Together with the Option Exercise Fee, the total deposit cannot exceed S$5,000. Neither fee can be zero; the deposit forms part of the resale price.

Must both parties submit the resale application on the same day?

No, but the second party must submit within seven calendar days of the first party's submission. Separately, agree the submission timeframe in the OTP. If the second party misses it, the application lapses and both parties must submit a new application and pay the fees again.

Can I stay in my HDB flat after resale completion?

Only if you qualify for a temporary extension of stay, the buyer agrees and HDB approves the arrangement. The extension is at most three months after completion and is not automatic. It does not postpone the legal sale.

Valuation and price

When does HDB valuation happen?

The buyer’s Request for Value follows the OTP. Buyers using CPF or a housing loan must submit it by the next working day after the Option Date, unless their financial institution advises otherwise. HDB decides whether a valuation is required.

How much does an HDB valuation cost, and who pays?

The buyer pays HDB a $120 processing fee, including GST, for a Request for Value; the seller does not pay it. The fee is non-refundable. The result applies to that OTP and is valid for three months from availability in the HDB Flat Portal; the resale application must be submitted within that period.

Can a seller get an official HDB valuation before listing?

Not through HDB’s resale Request for Value process: the buyer needs a granted OTP. Before listing, use comparable HDB resale transactions to discuss an indicative price range with an agent.

What is Cash Over Valuation (COV)?

COV is the amount by which the agreed purchase price exceeds valuation. It must be paid in cash, without CPF or a housing loan. At a price of $600,000 and valuation of $588,000, COV is $12,000. A price at or below valuation has no COV, but other cash costs may still apply.

Why is my flat's valuation lower than recent sales nearby?

Nearby transactions may differ in flat size, remaining lease, storey, condition and location. An asking price is also different from a registered sale price. Compare similar completed sales rather than assuming the highest nearby price applies to your flat.

Agent commission

What is the property agent commission for selling an HDB flat in Singapore?

There is no fixed HDB seller agent fee. HDB says sellers may manage the sale themselves or engage a salesperson for a fee, and should agree the commission, service terms, representation period, exclusivity and advertising expenses. Joe Tay's quoted full-service seller fee is 2%, subject to a written scope.

Is condo seller agent commission fixed in Singapore?

There is no official fixed condo seller commission. CEA says commission rates are negotiable. Compare each proposal by the agreed percentage or amount, included services, separate expenses, GST treatment and agency terms.

Who pays a dedicated buyer's agent in Singapore?

If you appoint a dedicated buyer's agent, agree the fee and scope in a CEA Prescribed Estate Agency Agreement. The agent represents and collects commission from you and cannot also represent or collect commission from the seller in the same transaction. Luxury or off-market searches do not have a separate CEA-prescribed fee structure.

When is property agent commission paid, and is GST added?

CEA says the agreed commission is paid after the property transaction is completed and should be paid to the property agency, not directly to the salesperson. Clarify whether GST is included or excluded; only GST-registered property agencies can charge GST.

Do you pay an agent fee if you found the property on 99.co?

A listing enquiry alone does not appoint an agent to represent you. Ask whose interests the agent represents and agree any fee before appointing your own agent. There is no universal 99.co agent commission rate.

Selling a condo

How long does it take to sell a condo in Singapore?

Once a buyer exercises the Option to Purchase, completion is commonly set 8 to 12 weeks later, and both sides can agree a different period. Finding the buyer comes before that and depends on price, condition and demand in your project.

Do I need to meet an MOP before selling a private condo?

No. Private condos have no Minimum Occupation Period. Check Seller’s Stamp Duty instead: it applies if you sell within four years of a purchase made on or after 4 July 2025, or within three years of one made between 11 March 2017 and 3 July 2025. Executive condominiums have their own occupation rules.

Do I have to refund CPF when I sell my condo?

Yes. CPF Board requires the principal you withdrew and its accrued interest to be refunded to your own CPF account. If the proceeds are not enough to cover it, you do not need to top up the shortfall in cash provided the property is sold at market value.

Is the option fee for a private property fixed?

No. For a private sale the option fee, the option period and the exercise deposit are agreed between seller and buyer. A 1% option fee, a 14-day option and a further 4% on exercise are market conventions, not rules.

Seller’s Stamp Duty

How much is Seller’s Stamp Duty in Singapore?

For a home bought on or after 4 July 2025, SSD is 16% if sold within one year, 12% in the second year, 8% in the third and 4% in the fourth. For a home bought between 11 March 2017 and 3 July 2025 it is 12%, 8% and 4% over three years. It is charged on the higher of the selling price and market value.

How long must I hold a property to avoid SSD?

More than four years if you bought on or after 4 July 2025, and more than three years if you bought between 11 March 2017 and 3 July 2025. IRAS counts from the date of purchase to the date of sale, which is usually the date the option is accepted or the agreement is signed, not the completion date.

Who pays Seller’s Stamp Duty, and when is it due?

The seller pays. IRAS requires payment within 14 days of the sale contract being executed and says payment cannot be deferred. Your conveyancing lawyer normally arranges it from the sale proceeds.

Does Seller’s Stamp Duty apply to HDB flats?

Yes. IRAS says meeting the Minimum Occupation Period does not remove SSD if the sale falls within the holding period. Because the usual five-year MOP is longer than the SSD holding period, the two rarely overlap in practice.

Buyer’s stamp duties

How much is ABSD for a Singapore Citizen buying a second property?

20% of the purchase price or market value, whichever is higher. A Singapore Citizen's first residential property is exempt from ABSD (0%); a third or subsequent property is charged at 30%.

How much is ABSD for a Permanent Resident?

5% on a Permanent Resident's first residential property, 30% on the second, and 35% on the third and subsequent properties.

How much is ABSD for a foreigner buying property in Singapore?

60% of the purchase price or market value, whichever is higher, regardless of whether it is the buyer's first property in Singapore. Nationals of the US, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway and Switzerland are treated as Singapore Citizens for stamp duty under Singapore's Free Trade Agreements.

Is Buyer's Stamp Duty (BSD) different for HDB and private property?

No. BSD uses the same tiered rate table — 1% to 6% depending on price — for both HDB resale flats and private residential property. ABSD, where it applies, is calculated on top of BSD.

How much stamp duty do I pay on a second property in Singapore?

Buyer's Stamp Duty at the usual tiered rates, plus ABSD: 20% for a Singapore Citizen and 30% for a Permanent Resident. A Singapore Citizen buying a second property at $1,500,000 pays $44,600 BSD and $300,000 ABSD, $344,600 in total.

When must stamp duty be paid?

Within 14 days after signing the document if it is signed in Singapore, or within 30 days after receiving it if it is signed overseas. For most purchases that document is the exercised Option to Purchase or the Sale and Purchase Agreement. Late payment attracts penalties.

Renting out your home

Can I rent out my whole HDB flat?

Only if you are a Singapore Citizen owner who has met the Minimum Occupation Period, and only with HDB’s approval. Singapore Permanent Resident owners may not rent out a whole flat, and HDB says owners of Prime and Plus flats may not do so even after their 10-year MOP.

What is the minimum rental period in Singapore?

For an HDB flat, each tenant must rent for at least 6 months per application. For a private home, URA requires every occupant to stay at least three consecutive months. Short-term letting is not allowed in either.

How many tenants can I have?

In an HDB flat: 4 in a 1-room or 2-room flat, 6 in a 3-room flat and 8 in a 4-room or bigger flat, reverting to 6 for a rental period that extends beyond 31 December 2028. In a private home smaller than 90 square metres the cap is six unrelated persons; larger homes may house eight until 31 December 2028 if registered with URA.

What is the non-citizen quota for HDB rentals?

It is 8% at the neighbourhood level and 11% at the block level, and it applies when any tenant of a whole flat is a non-Malaysian non-citizen. Once the quota is reached, only Singaporeans and Malaysians can rent a whole flat there. It does not apply to the rental of bedrooms.

Is rental income taxable in Singapore?

Yes. Net rental income is taxable. You may deduct actual expenses such as mortgage interest, property tax, fire insurance and maintenance, or for a residential property claim deemed expenses of 15% of gross rent plus mortgage interest.

Rents and yields

Are HDB rents going up in 2026?

Slowly. The median 4-room HDB rent was $3,400 a month in 3Q 2026, the same as in 2Q and up from $3,300 a year earlier, and 19 of 25 towns with a 4-room figure were higher than a year before. HDB approved 9,381 applications to rent out flats in the quarter, 6.2% fewer than in 2Q.

What is the average rent for a 4-room HDB flat?

The median across all 4-room rentals HDB approved in July to September 2026 was $3,400 a month. Town medians ranged from $3,000 in Bukit Panjang to $4,800 in the Central Area.

Which HDB towns have the highest rental yield?

Dividing 3Q 2026 median rent by median resale price, 4-room flats yielded most in Jurong West (about 8.0% gross) and Jurong East (7.8%), and least in Toa Payoh (4.5%) and Queenstown (4.8%). These are gross figures before costs and vacancy.

Did private condo rents rise in 3Q 2026?

URA’s private rental index rose 1.3% in 3Q 2026. Non-landed rents rose 3.1% in the suburbs and 1.5% in the city fringe and fell 0.3% in the city centre, while vacancy of completed private homes rose to 7.2%.
