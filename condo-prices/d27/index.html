Common questions

What is the median condo resale price in District 27?

The median resale price for private condominiums and apartments in District 27 (Yishun, Sembawang, Admiralty) is $1,280,000, based on 313 caveats lodged in the 12 months to 2026-08.

What is the median price per square foot (psf) for condos in District 27?

The median price is $1296 psf across resale condominiums and apartments, based on caveats lodged in the 12 months to 2026-08.

Have condo prices in District 27 gone up or down over the past year?

Like for like, condo resale prices in District 27 have risen 2.4% over the past year. This compares the median price per square foot of the same unit sizes and tenures in the 12 months to 2026-08 against the prior 12 months, so a year when bigger or newer units sold does not count as a price rise.
