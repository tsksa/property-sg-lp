Common questions

What is the median condo resale price in District 15?

The median resale price for private condominiums and apartments in District 15 (East Coast, Marine Parade, Katong) is $2,140,000, based on 1027 caveats lodged in the 12 months to 2026-09.

What is the median price per square foot (psf) for condos in District 15?

The median price is $1833 psf across resale condominiums and apartments, based on caveats lodged in the 12 months to 2026-09.

Have condo prices in District 15 gone up or down over the past year?

Like for like, condo resale prices in District 15 have risen 3.7% over the past year. This compares the median price per square foot of the same unit sizes and tenures in the 12 months to 2026-09 against the prior 12 months, so a year when bigger or newer units sold does not count as a price rise.

Which condos in District 15 have the highest $psf?

Among projects with at least 3 resale caveats in the 12 months to 2026-09, the highest median prices per square foot were AMBER PARK ($2,913 psf, 28 sales), MEYER MANSION ($2,899 psf, 14 sales) and ONE MEYER ($2,812 psf, 4 sales). These medians are not adjusted for unit size, floor or remaining lease.

What is the cheapest condo in District 15 by $psf?

Among projects with at least 3 resale caveats in the 12 months to 2026-09, NEPTUNE COURT had the lowest median at $1,100 psf across 23 sales. The median is not adjusted for unit size, floor or remaining lease.

Are freehold condos in District 15 more expensive?

In the 12 months to 2026-09, freehold, 999- and 9999-year resale condos had a median $1,894 psf across 696 caveats, against $1,667 psf across 313 caveats for 99-year leasehold: 14% higher for freehold. The two groups differ in age, location and unit size, so this is not a like-for-like premium.
