Common questions

What is the median condo resale price in District 10?

The median resale price for private condominiums and apartments in District 10 (Bukit Timah, Holland, Tanglin) is $3,180,000, based on 826 caveats lodged in the 12 months to 2026-09.

What is the median price per square foot (psf) for condos in District 10?

The median price is $2239 psf across resale condominiums and apartments, based on caveats lodged in the 12 months to 2026-09.

Have condo prices in District 10 gone up or down over the past year?

Like for like, condo resale prices in District 10 have risen 1.5% over the past year. This compares the median price per square foot of the same unit sizes and tenures in the 12 months to 2026-09 against the prior 12 months, so a year when bigger or newer units sold does not count as a price rise.

Which condos in District 10 have the highest $psf?

Among projects with at least 3 resale caveats in the 12 months to 2026-09, the highest median prices per square foot were SCULPTURA ARDMORE ($6,112 psf, 3 sales), ARDMORE PARK ($4,290 psf, 6 sales) and BOULEVARD 88 ($4,030 psf, 9 sales). These medians are not adjusted for unit size, floor or remaining lease.

What is the cheapest condo in District 10 by $psf?

Among projects with at least 3 resale caveats in the 12 months to 2026-09, LUTHERAN TOWERS had the lowest median at $1,137 psf across 4 sales. The median is not adjusted for unit size, floor or remaining lease.

Are freehold condos in District 10 more expensive?

In the 12 months to 2026-09, freehold, 999- and 9999-year resale condos had a median $2,302 psf across 636 caveats, against $2,046 psf across 182 caveats for 99-year leasehold: 13% higher for freehold. The two groups differ in age, location and unit size, so this is not a like-for-like premium.
