Common questions

What is the median condo resale price in District 21?

The median resale price for private condominiums and apartments in District 21 (Upper Bukit Timah, Clementi Park, Ulu Pandan) is $1,980,000, based on 419 caveats lodged in the 12 months to 2026-09.

What is the median price per square foot (psf) for condos in District 21?

The median price is $1831 psf across resale condominiums and apartments, based on caveats lodged in the 12 months to 2026-09.

Have condo prices in District 21 gone up or down over the past year?

Like for like, condo resale prices in District 21 have risen 2.0% over the past year. This compares the median price per square foot of the same unit sizes and tenures in the 12 months to 2026-09 against the prior 12 months, so a year when bigger or newer units sold does not count as a price rise.

Which condos in District 21 have the highest $psf?

Among projects with at least 3 resale caveats in the 12 months to 2026-09, the highest median prices per square foot were FLORIDIAN ($2,458 psf, 7 sales), THE STERLING ($2,350 psf, 4 sales) and THE NEXUS ($2,349 psf, 9 sales). These medians are not adjusted for unit size, floor or remaining lease.

What is the cheapest condo in District 21 by $psf?

Among projects with at least 3 resale caveats in the 12 months to 2026-09, PINE GROVE had the lowest median at $1,047 psf across 10 sales. The median is not adjusted for unit size, floor or remaining lease.

Are freehold condos in District 21 more expensive?

In the 12 months to 2026-09, freehold, 999- and 9999-year resale condos had a median $1,800 psf across 275 caveats, against $1,943 psf across 130 caveats for 99-year leasehold: 7% lower for freehold. The two groups differ in age, location and unit size, so this is not a like-for-like premium.
