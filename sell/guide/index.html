The seller’s guide: selling a home in Singapore, step by step

Every selling guide on this site, in the order an owner needs them. Each one cites the official HDB, CPF Board, IRAS, URA or CEA page it relies on.

By Joe Tay · District Director, ERA Realty Network · CEA R009618D

1. Know what your home is worth

Price decides how long a sale takes and how much you keep. Start with registered sales, not asking prices.

2. Check that you can sell, and what it triggers

An HDB flat must meet its Minimum Occupation Period. Any home sold soon after purchase may attract Seller’s Stamp Duty.

3. Plan the timeline

HDB’s eight weeks starts only when it accepts the resale application. The whole sale takes longer.
