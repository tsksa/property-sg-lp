Renting a home in Singapore, with an agent acting for you

Most rental listings are advertised by the landlord’s agent, who works for the landlord. Joe can act for you instead: shortlisting homes, checking the home can lawfully be let to you and negotiating the tenancy.

By Joe Tay · District Director, ERA Realty Network · CEA R009618D
The short answer

An HDB flat must be rented for at least 6 months and needs HDB’s approval before you move in. A private home must be rented for at least 3 consecutive months. Either way the tenancy agreement is stamped: IRAS charges 0.4% of the total rent for a lease of up to four years, and the agreement normally makes the tenant pay it.

Who works for you when you rent

The agent advertising a rental normally represents the landlord. An agent can collect commission from only one party in the same transaction, so if you want someone negotiating for you, appoint your own tenant’s agent. The party who appoints the agent pays them. There is no fixed rate; Joe quotes rental work case by case and agrees it in writing before viewings start.

What Joe does for a tenant

  • Shortlist. Finds homes in your budget and area, using recent rents for the same flat or unit type to tell you what is fair.
  • Check the rules. For an HDB flat, confirms the owner may rent it out and that you, and everyone moving in with you, fit HDB’s tenant and quota rules. For a condo, checks the occupancy cap.
  • Viewings and offer. Arranges viewings, then negotiates the rent, term, deposit, repairs and early-termination terms with the landlord’s agent.
  • Tenancy agreement. Goes through the agreement with you before you sign, gets it stamped on time and records the condition of the home at handover.

Renting a whole HDB flat

  • Who may rent. Singapore Citizens, Singapore Permanent Residents, and non-citizens legally in Singapore on an Employment Pass, S Pass, Work Permit, Student Pass, Dependant Pass or Long-Term Social Visit Pass with at least 6 months’ validity. Tourists may not rent an HDB flat.
  • Minimum period. At least 6 months per tenant; short-term letting is not allowed. The owner must have HDB’s approval before you move in.
  • Non-citizen quota. If any tenant is a non-Malaysian non-citizen, the block and neighbourhood quota applies: 8% for the neighbourhood and 11% for the block. Once it is full, only Singaporeans and Malaysians can rent a whole flat there.
  • How many people. Up to 4 tenants in a 1-room or 2-room flat, 6 in a 3-room flat and 8 in a 4-room or bigger flat, reverting to 6 for a rental period that extends beyond 31 December 2028. The cap does not apply if all the tenants are one family unit.

Sources: HDB, eligibility for renting out a flat and regulations for renting out a flat.

Renting a condo or private home

No government approval is needed, but URA’s rules apply. Every occupant must stay at least three consecutive months. A home smaller than 90 square metres may house up to six unrelated persons; a larger home registered with URA may house up to eight until 31 December 2028. The development’s by-laws also bind you, for example on move-in and renovation hours. Source: URA, renting property.

The tenancy agreement and stamp duty

The agreement should state the rent, the term, the deposit, who pays for repairs and how either side may end the lease early. It is then stamped. IRAS charges lease duty at 0.4% of the total rent for a lease of four years or less, and leases with an average annual rent of $1,000 or less are exempt. Duty is due within 14 days of signing if the agreement is signed in Singapore. Sources: IRAS, stamp duty rates and when to pay stamp duty.

Worked example

A two-year lease at $3,400 a month has a total rent of $81,600. Lease duty is 0.4% × $81,600 = $326, rounded down to the nearest dollar.

Common questions

Do tenants pay agent commission in Singapore?

Only if they appoint their own agent. The agent advertising a rental normally represents the landlord, and an agent can collect commission from only one party in the same transaction. There is no fixed rate.

What is the minimum rental period?

At least 6 months per tenant for a whole HDB flat, and at least three consecutive months for a private home. Short-term letting is not allowed in either.

Who pays the stamp duty on a tenancy agreement?

The tenancy agreement normally makes the tenant responsible. IRAS charges 0.4% of the total rent for a lease of four years or less, due within 14 days of signing in Singapore.

Can a foreigner rent a whole HDB flat?

Yes, on an Employment Pass, S Pass, Work Permit, Student Pass, Dependant Pass or Long-Term Social Visit Pass with at least 6 months’ validity, subject to the block’s non-citizen quota. Tourists may not.
