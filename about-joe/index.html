How I work

One advisor from the first number to completion.

I work directly with every client. That means the person who studies the recent transactions, sets the pricing strategy, handles viewings and negotiates the offer is the same person you speak to throughout.

My approach starts with evidence: comparable transactions, the condition and position of the property, current competing supply, financing constraints and the owner’s actual timeline. I’ll explain the trade-offs plainly—even when waiting or changing the plan is the better answer.

I advise across HDB, condominium, landed and commercial property, with direct support in English and Mandarin.
