How onboarding works

Submit agent details.
Use the public agent form with your name, email, CEA number, agency and WhatsApp contact.
HomeLah checks the CEA register.
The automated check confirms the CEA number status; it is not treated as identity proof by itself.
Operator approval unlocks the dashboard.
Once approved, you can create listings, manage leads and use free starter credits for your first boost.

What is not automated yet

WhatsApp OTP, bulk invites, live Stripe activation and paid alert email delivery remain rollout items that should be enabled only after the operating approvals and production keys are ready.
