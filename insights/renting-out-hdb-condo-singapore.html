Renting out an HDB flat or condo in Singapore: the rules, the tax and the steps

HDB flats and private homes are let under different rules. HDB decides who may rent out a flat and to whom; URA sets the minimum stay and occupancy cap for private homes.

By Joe Tay · District Director, ERA Realty Network · CEA R009618D

The short answer

To rent out a whole HDB flat you must be a Singapore Citizen, have met the Minimum Occupation Period and have HDB’s approval; each tenant must stay at least 6 months. A private home needs no approval, but every occupant must stay at least 3 consecutive months. Either way the lease is stamped and the net rent is taxable.

Renting out a whole HDB flat

Who may rent out

HDB allows only Singapore Citizen owners to rent out a whole flat; Singapore Permanent Resident owners may not. You must first meet the 5-year Minimum Occupation Period. For a non-subsidised flat bought on the open market before 30 August 2010 it is 3 years. HDB says owners of Prime and Plus flats are not allowed to rent out the whole flat even after their 10-year MOP.

Approval and rental period

You need HDB’s written approval, applied for online, before the tenants move in, and you must reapply each time you rent out the flat again or renew. The minimum rental period is 6 months for each tenant; short-term letting is not allowed. Approval runs for up to 3 years if every tenant is Singaporean or Malaysian, and up to 2 years if any tenant is a non-Malaysian non-citizen.

Who may be your tenant

Tenants must be Singapore Citizens, Singapore Permanent Residents, or non-citizens legally residing in Singapore on an Employment Pass, S Pass, Work Permit, Student Pass, Dependant Pass or Long-Term Social Visit Pass with at least 6 months’ validity at the date of application. HDB adds that Work Permit holders from the construction, manufacturing, marine and process sectors must be Malaysians, and that tourists may not be tenants.

Non-citizen quota

If any tenant is a non-Malaysian non-citizen, the non-citizen quota applies: 8% at the neighbourhood level and 11% at the block level. Once it is reached, only Singaporeans and Malaysians can rent a whole flat there. HDB has an e-service to check the quota for your block before you advertise.

How many tenants

Flat typeMaximum number of tenants
1-room and 2-room4
3-room6
4-room and bigger8, reverting to 6 for a rental period that extends beyond 31 December 2028

HDB says the maximum does not apply if all the tenants renting a whole flat are from the same family unit.

Your responsibilities as owner

HDB holds the owner responsible for what happens in the flat. You must make sure only the authorised tenants live there, that they are legally residing in Singapore, that they use it only as a home and that they do not sublet it. If you live overseas during the rental, you must appoint an attorney to manage the flat, and that attorney cannot be one of the tenants.

Renting out bedrooms while you continue to live in the flat follows a separate set of rules; see HDB’s renting out bedrooms pages.

Renting out a private condo or house

No government approval is needed to let a private home, but URA’s rules apply to the owner:

  • Minimum stay. Every occupant must stay at least three consecutive months. Renting it out as short-term accommodation is not allowed.
  • Occupancy cap. A home smaller than 90 square metres may house up to six unrelated persons. A home of at least 90 square metres may house up to eight until 31 December 2028, if the owner registers it with URA and pays the $20 fee.
  • Subletting. The cap applies to tenants who sublet as well, and URA says the owner must ensure tenants follow the rules.

Check your development’s by-laws too. The management corporation can set house rules on move-ins, renovation hours and use of facilities that your tenant must follow.

The tenancy agreement and stamp duty

A written tenancy agreement should state the rent, the term, the deposit, who pays for repairs and how either side may end the lease early. It is then stamped. IRAS charges lease duty at 0.4% of the total rent for a lease of four years or less, and leases with an average annual rent of $1,000 or less are exempt. The agreement normally makes the tenant responsible for paying it. Stamp duty is due within 14 days of signing if the document is signed in Singapore.

Worked example

A two-year lease at $3,400 a month has a total rent of $81,600. Lease duty is 0.4% × $81,600 = $326, rounded down to the nearest dollar.

Tax on rental income

Net rental income is taxable and is declared in your own income tax return. IRAS taxes rent from the date it is due, not the date you receive it. You may deduct expenses incurred to produce the rent, such as mortgage interest, property tax, fire insurance, maintenance and the cost of securing a tenant.

For a residential property you can instead claim deemed expenses of 15% of the gross rent, and still claim mortgage interest on top. You cannot mix the two methods for the same property. Renovation, additions and initial repairs are not deductible. Keep the tenancy agreement, loan statements and receipts for at least 5 years.

Property tax changes too. Owner-occupier rates apply only to a home you live in, and HDB notes that the property tax on a flat is revised when it is rented out. IRAS publishes the current property tax rates.

Steps to rent out your home

  1. Confirm you are allowed to: citizenship and MOP for an HDB flat, and the non-citizen quota for your block.
  2. Set the rent from recent rentals in your town or project. The rental market report has the median HDB rent for every town.
  3. Check each prospective tenant’s identity and pass, and that the number of occupants is within the cap.
  4. Sign the tenancy agreement and have it stamped.
  5. For an HDB flat, obtain HDB’s approval before the tenants move in.
  6. Hand over with a dated inventory and photographs, and note the dates for renewal and for your tax return.

Frequently asked questions

Can I rent out my whole HDB flat?

Only if you are a Singapore Citizen owner who has met the Minimum Occupation Period, and only with HDB’s approval. Singapore Permanent Resident owners may not rent out a whole flat, and HDB says owners of Prime and Plus flats may not do so even after their 10-year MOP.

What is the minimum rental period in Singapore?

For an HDB flat, each tenant must rent for at least 6 months per application. For a private home, URA requires every occupant to stay at least three consecutive months. Short-term letting is not allowed in either.

How many tenants can I have?

In an HDB flat: 4 in a 1-room or 2-room flat, 6 in a 3-room flat and 8 in a 4-room or bigger flat, reverting to 6 for a rental period that extends beyond 31 December 2028. In a private home smaller than 90 square metres the cap is six unrelated persons; larger homes may house eight until 31 December 2028 if registered with URA.

What is the non-citizen quota for HDB rentals?

It is 8% at the neighbourhood level and 11% at the block level, and it applies when any tenant of a whole flat is a non-Malaysian non-citizen. Once the quota is reached, only Singaporeans and Malaysians can rent a whole flat there. It does not apply to the rental of bedrooms.

Is rental income taxable in Singapore?

Yes. Net rental income is taxable. You may deduct actual expenses such as mortgage interest, property tax, fire insurance and maintenance, or for a residential property claim deemed expenses of 15% of gross rent plus mortgage interest.

This guide is general education, not legal, tax or financial advice. IRAS, HDB, URA and CPF Board apply the prevailing rules to the facts of your own case; check the official page or ask a conveyancing lawyer before you commit.
