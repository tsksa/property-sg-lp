Selling HDB after MOP: the 5-step timeline with CPF & HDB paperwork

Before you sell, confirm your eligibility, separate cash proceeds from CPF refunds, and line up the next home. These five steps show what to check and when, with a worked sale example.

Can you sell once MOP ends?

You can proceed only after HDB confirms you meet the applicable selling conditions. Before granting an Option to Purchase (OTP), check your MOP, register an Intent to Sell, work out your cash and CPF separately, and plan where you will live next.

This guide is for an owner preparing a resale after MOP. Use it to collect the dates and figures you need; your HDB records, CPF statements and conveyancing advice decide your actual position.

Step 1 — Confirm MOP has actually cleared

For a new flat, MOP generally starts at key collection; for a resale purchase, it starts at resale completion. It is an occupation requirement, not simply the time since booking. Periods when you did not occupy the flat can be excluded.

Five years is common for existing flats and Standard flats. Plus and Prime flats, including PLH flats, have a 10-year MOP and additional conditions. Special purchase schemes can differ. HDB's flat-classification guide explains these distinctions: confirm your own recorded eligibility through HDB before committing to a sale.

  1. Check your flat details and MOP status through HDB's signed-in services. Flag periods of non-occupation and any ownership changes.
  2. Register an Intent to Sell on My Flat Dashboard. It is free and valid for 12 months. Wait at least seven days after registration before granting an OTP.
  3. Check the Ethnic Integration Policy and SPR quota, co-owner agreement, and any outstanding upgrading charges. MOP alone does not confirm every selling condition.

Step 2 — Work out your net proceeds (especially CPF refund)

Write down three separate figures: the housing loan to redeem, the required CPF refund for all owners, and the cash left after selling costs. CPF Board's sale-proceeds guidance puts the outstanding loan before the required CPF refund, followed by other sale expenses for HDB flats.

Get a loan redemption estimate for your expected completion date and each owner's required refund from CPF's Home ownership dashboard. The refund includes CPF principal used, including housing grants, and accrued interest. Accrued interest reflects the interest those savings would have earned in OA, currently 2.5% a year. The amount depends on each withdrawal and its timing; use the dashboard figure rather than applying a flat amount to your original purchase price.

Worked example: a $600,000 sale is not $600,000 of spending money

Illustrative assumptions, not a client result or fee quote: a 4-room flat sells for $600,000, the loan redemption amount is $150,000, and the combined required CPF refund is $200,000. Both owners are below 55. The sale fully covers the loan and CPF refund. Assume no resale levy, subsidy recovery, loan penalty or other outstanding charges.

ItemAmount (S$)
Sale price, including deposit$600,000
Outstanding loan to redeem$150,000
Combined CPF refund$200,000
Commission including GST$13,080
Legal-fee budget$2,500
Seller's HDB application fee$80
Total net cash from the sale$234,340
Deposit already received$5,000
Cash still to receive after these costs$229,340

The calculation is $600,000 − $150,000 − $200,000 − $13,080 − $2,500 − $80 = $234,340. The $5,000 deposit is part of that total, not extra income: $5,000 already received + $229,340 still to receive = $234,340. This is an overall budget; actual payment dates and the completion statement depend on when each bill is paid.

The example assumes 2% commission plus 9% GST: $12,000 + $1,080. Commission is negotiable, not an official fixed rate; use your signed agency agreement and check GST treatment. The $2,500 legal allowance is a budgeting assumption, not a tariff. HDB's seller application fee is $80 for 3-room or larger flats and $40 for 1- or 2-room flats. See the commission guide and HDB application fees.

The $200,000 returned to CPF is separate from the $234,340 cash. It is not a profit calculation either: the original purchase cost, past interest, renovation and holding expenses are not accounted for here. Before using the proceeds for another home, reserve moving and temporary-housing costs and check when funds will actually be available. The CPF purchase example shows how usable CPF and cash fit into a next purchase.

Two situations that need a different calculation

  • Selling at 55 or older: CPF refunds first top up your Retirement Account to your applicable Full Retirement Sum. Check what remains usable for housing; do not assume the entire refund returns to spendable OA savings. See CPF's guide for members above 55.
  • Sale proceeds cannot cover the required CPF refund: where the flat is sold at market value, CPF's shortfall rules can limit the refund to proceeds remaining after the loan, without a cash top-up of that CPF shortfall. This does not waive selling bills or loan obligations. Option monies already received still form part of the proceeds and may have to be returned. Below-market sales need separate assessment. Read CPF's shortfall guidance before relying on the simple example.

Step 3 — Get an honest indicative valuation

Compare your block's sold prices by flat type, size, storey range, lease and transaction date. If your block has few recent sales, widen the comparison and state that limitation. Asking prices are not completed-sale evidence.

An agent's price range is an estimate, not HDB's official valuation or a guaranteed sale price. Read how HDB valuation works and ask for the comparable transactions behind any proposed listing price. Use a lower-price scenario in your proceeds budget before committing to the next home.

Step 4 — Launch, negotiate, sign OTP

Use HDB's prescribed OTP after the Intent to Sell cooling-off period. The buyer has 21 calendar days to exercise it. The option fee is capped at $1,000 and the combined option and exercise fees at S$5,000. Check the buyer's valid HFE and agree the application deadline in the OTP.

Before accepting an offer, compare the price, financing readiness, proposed completion and your move-out plan. Discuss any stay extension early and confirm HDB's requirements; it is not an automatic right. For help arranging pricing, viewings and the resale steps, see Joe's HDB selling service.

Buying a condo first? Check ABSD before signing

A six-month sale is not an automatic ABSD refund. The married-couple remission requires, among other conditions, at least one Singapore citizen spouse and purchase under both spouses' names only. The deadline for selling your first home runs from purchase of a completed second home, or from TOP or CSC, whichever is earlier, for an uncompleted second home. A separate refund-application deadline applies after selling the first home.

Have your lawyer check all IRAS remission conditions, HDB ownership rules and upfront funding. Compare the routes in sell HDB first or buy condo first.

Selling an HDB flat after MOP does not by itself trigger a blanket 30-month wait. Private-property disposal and subsidised purchase routes have separate rules. HDB's July 2026 wait-out announcement concerns private-property owners; check your HFE, subsidy history and any resale levy for your next HDB purchase.

Step 5 — Submit resale, endorse online, attend completion

Meet the application deadline agreed in the exercised OTP. Buyer and seller submit their portions separately, within seven calendar days of each other. That interval is not a universal requirement to submit within seven days of exercising the OTP.

HDB's application guidance allows up to 28 working days to notify acceptance when the complete application and supporting documents are in order. The approximately eight-week processing period starts at acceptance, not when you list or grant the OTP. Endorse the documents and pay fees by the dates HDB gives you.

Agree completion, moving and any approved extension together. Confirm loan redemption, CPF refunds and cash disbursement with your conveyancer; do not promise a next-home payment using money that has not arrived. The HDB resale timeline guide separates marketing from the formal process. A whole-sale deadline cannot be guaranteed.

Before granting an OTP: your seller checklist

  • Eligibility: HDB-confirmed MOP status, co-owner agreement, buyer eligibility and current EIP/SPR quota.
  • Timing: valid Intent to Sell, cooling-off period complete, proposed completion and housing between homes.
  • Money: loan redemption estimate, each owner's CPF refund, written selling fees and any other liabilities.
  • Next home: HFE or financing checks, cash needed upfront, and tax advice if buying before selling.
  • Documents: prescribed OTP, agreed submission deadline, and supporting records requested in My Flat Dashboard.

Bring these figures to a seller planning discussion. You can review the plan without granting an OTP or committing to an asking price.

Frequently asked questions

When does the 5-year MOP start?

For a new flat, MOP generally starts at key collection; for a resale flat, at resale completion. Periods of non-occupation can be excluded. The applicable period depends on the flat and purchase conditions, so confirm HDB's recorded eligibility date rather than adding five years yourself.

How much CPF do I need to refund when I sell my HDB flat?

Use the required housing refund shown for each owner in CPF's Home ownership dashboard, including principal and accrued interest. Refunds remain CPF savings, not cash sale proceeds. For owners aged 55 and above, refunds first top up the Retirement Account to the applicable Full Retirement Sum.

Can I buy a condo before selling my HDB flat?

Check HDB ownership conditions, financing and ABSD before committing. Selling within six months does not automatically qualify you for an ABSD refund. The married-couple remission has separate citizenship, joint-ownership, sale-deadline and application conditions; the deadline differs for completed and uncompleted purchases.

How long does the HDB resale process take after the OTP?

Allow for the buyer's 21-calendar-day option period, the agreed application deadline, HDB's acceptance checks and about eight weeks from acceptance to completion. The buyer and seller must submit their portions within seven calendar days of each other. Marketing and any approved stay extension are separate.

Do I have to wait 30 months after selling my HDB flat?

Selling an HDB flat after MOP does not by itself trigger a blanket 30-month wait. Private-property disposal, subsidy history, resale levy and the next purchase route have separate rules. Obtain an HFE assessment for the next HDB purchase rather than assuming your present sale makes you eligible.
