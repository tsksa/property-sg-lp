The short version

13 condo and EC projects with about 7,047 homes are expected to launch between September 2026 and the end of 2027. Amberwood at Holland's booking day was set for 26 September 2026, and Lucerne Grand at Lakeside is expected in October. Six more projects are expected in the fourth quarter of 2026, from Thomson Reserve's 1,268 homes to Keppel Bay Plot 6's 84. The first quarter of 2027 brings Chuan Grove and three ECs, and Hougang Central Residences follows in late 2027. Launch windows are the developers' own guidance, and they move. Each project page updates when a date is confirmed.

The fourth quarter of 2026

From late September to December, about 4,165 homes are expected across eight projects, spread across every region:

  • City centre (CCR): Amberwood at Holland in Holland and The Serra Residences, a 133-home freehold project on Bassein Road. These are small projects for owner-occupiers and investors who want a central address.
  • City fringe (RCR): Thomson Reserve at Bright Hill, the largest launch of the year, Dorset Road in District 8, and Keppel Bay Plot 6 on Keppel Island.
  • Suburbs (OCR): Lucerne Grand near Lakeside MRT, Chencharu Close in District 27 and Upper Thomson Road. These are the main options for HDB upgraders buying new outside the city.

2027: Chuan Grove, three ECs and Hougang Central

The first quarter of 2027 is expected to bring about 2,052 homes. Chuan Grove has 1,055 of them, at Chuan Grove off Lorong Chuan. The other three are ECs with 997 homes between them: Solano Grand in Bukit Panjang, Wynwood Grand in Woodlands and Sembawang Road EC. All three keep the $16,000 household income ceiling, because their sites were tendered before the 24 August 2026 cut-off. EC vs private condo in 2027 explains the ceiling and compares prices.

Hougang Central Residences, 830 homes above Hougang MRT, is guided for late 2027. Singapore's 2027 mega launches covers the wider 2027 pipeline and how upgraders should time a sale around it.

How to time a purchase around the calendar

  • Sort out your HDB sale first. Most buyers of these projects are selling an HDB flat. A married couple who are Singapore citizens can buy first, pay ABSD and get it refunded if they sell the flat within six months of the new condo's TOP or CSC, whichever is earlier. The deadline is strict. How long it takes to sell and selling after MOP set out the sale timeline.
  • Get your loan limit before the preview. Booking a unit takes a 5% booking fee on the day, so have a bank's in-principle loan approval ready. Run the affordability calculator and read MSR vs TDSR before you visit a show flat. HDB loan vs bank loan explains why new condos need a bank loan.
  • Compare with resale. Several suburban launches sit near older condos and large HDB estates. Check the condo prices by district and HDB resale prices by town before accepting a launch price per square foot.
  • Price the stamp duty. The stamp duty calculator works out buyer's stamp duty and any ABSD, which depends on what you already own when you buy.

Frequently asked questions

What new condo launches are coming in late 2026?

Amberwood at Holland’s booking day was set for 26 September 2026, and Lucerne Grand is expected in October 2026. Thomson Reserve, The Serra Residences, Dorset Road, Chencharu Close, Upper Thomson Road and Keppel Bay Plot 6 are expected in the fourth quarter of 2026, about 4,165 homes in all.

Which ECs are launching in 2027?

Three ECs are expected in the first quarter of 2027: Solano Grand (the Senja Close site) in Bukit Panjang, Wynwood Grand (Woodlands Drive 17) and Sembawang Road EC. All three keep the $16,000 income ceiling because their land tenders closed before 24 August 2026.

What is the biggest new launch in 2026?

Thomson Reserve at Bright Hill Drive, with 1,268 homes, is the largest project expected in 2026. In 2027, Chuan Grove (1,055 homes) and Hougang Central Residences (830 homes) are the largest.

Are new launch dates confirmed?

Only Amberwood at Holland has confirmed preview and booking dates. The other windows are developers’ guidance from announcements and results filings and can move. Each project page on this site updates when a preview or booking date is confirmed.
