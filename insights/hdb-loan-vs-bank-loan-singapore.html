HDB loan vs bank loan in 2026: interest rates, cash and which costs less

HDB charges 2.6% a year from 1 October to 31 December 2026. Some banks now advertise HDB-flat packages below that, from 1.78%. This guide shows what each loan costs on $450,000, the cash each needs, and what you give up for the lower rate.

Quick answer

HDB's loan rate is 2.6% a year from 1 October to 31 December 2026, set at 0.1 percentage point above the CPF Ordinary Account rate. Bank packages for HDB flats, as published on the banks' own websites on 30 Oct 2026, start lower: DBS advertises 1.78% for the first three years, and UOB advertises 3-month compounded SORA + 0.70%, about 1.97% at the latest SORA of 1.2748% (29 Oct 2026). On a $450,000 loan over 25 years, that is $140 to $182 a month less than HDB in the first years.

The trade-off: a bank loan needs at least 5% of the price in cash, is tested at 4% instead of HDB's 3% so it can approve a smaller loan, usually comes with a lock-in, and you can never move that flat back to an HDB loan.

HDB bank loan interest rate: how bank packages are priced

Banks price loans for HDB flats in three ways:

  • Fixed, then floating. The rate is fixed for one to three years, then moves to a floating formula. The formula after the fixed years matters more than the fixed rate, because it applies for most of the loan.
  • Pegged to SORA. The rate is 3-month compounded SORA plus the bank's spread. SORA is the Singapore Overnight Rate Average, published daily by MAS. The 3-month compounded figure averages the past three months, so it moves slowly. Ask how often the bank resets your rate.
  • Pegged to the CPF OA rate. The rate is a fixed margin below the CPF Ordinary Account rate, the same rate HDB adds 0.1 point to. DBS's package works this way. If the CPF OA rate changes, HDB's rate and the package rate move by the same amount, so the gap stays. The CPF OA rate has a legislated minimum of 2.5%, so DBS's year 1 to 3 rate cannot fall below 1.78% either.

The rate after the promotional years is called the thereafter rate. It is 2.40% on the DBS package and SORA + 1.00% on the UOB package. That is the rate you pay for most of a 25-year loan, so compare it with HDB's 2.6% before the year-one rate.

Whatever the package rate, the bank must check that you could pay at a higher rate. MAS sets this at the higher of 4% or the package's thereafter rate, within the 30% Mortgage Servicing Ratio and the 55% Total Debt Servicing Ratio. MSR vs TDSR explains both ratios.

Break-even with HDB's 2.6%: a package at SORA + 1.00% costs more than an HDB loan once SORA is above 1.60%. At SORA + 0.70%, the line is 1.90%. SORA is 1.27% now, 0.33 point below the first line. Worked example: $600,000 resale flat, $450,000 loan over 25 years

This is the same flat as the worked example in the home loan calculator, with two bank packages added. Assume a resale flat has both a $600,000 purchase price and a $600,000 valuation, the buyers qualify for 75% LTV, and a 25-year loan ends before they turn 65. The loan is $450,000 and the minimum downpayment is $150,000.

  • HDB loan: the $150,000 can come from CPF OA, cash or both. There is no minimum cash amount.
  • Bank loan: at least $30,000 (5% of the flat price) must be cash. The other $120,000 can be cash or CPF OA.
  • Either loan: stamp duty, legal fees and any cash-over-valuation come on top. If the price is above valuation, the difference is paid in cash and the loan is still capped at 75% of the valuation.

Assumptions: the CPF OA rate stays at 2.5%, 3-month SORA stays at 1.2748%, and the instalment is recalculated over the remaining term each time the rate changes, using the standard formula: instalment = loan × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)^−n), where r is the monthly rate and n the number of months. On those assumptions, over five years the DBS package costs about $14,088 less in interest than HDB and the UOB package about $10,492 less. Over the full 25 years, total interest comes to about $162,454 with HDB, $138,106 with DBS and $136,568 with UOB. Rates will not stay still for 25 years, so treat the long-run figures as a direction, not a forecast.

The bank saving has a price: $30,000 in cash up front, a lock-in, and no way back to HDB. If SORA rises above 1.60%, the UOB package costs more than HDB from year 4.

Stress test: why a bank may lend you less than HDB

Neither lender approves a loan on today's rate. HDB checks the 30% MSR at the higher of 3% or its own rate, which is 3% now. Banks use the higher of 4% or the package's thereafter rate, which is 4% for every package above. MAS raised its floor to 4% and HDB brought in its 3% floor from 30 September 2022.

Can you switch? HDB to bank yes, bank to HDB never

You can refinance an HDB loan to a bank at any time, subject to the bank's approval. You cannot go the other way: once a flat's loan is with a bank, CPF Board and HDB say it cannot be refinanced to an HDB loan. After a bank's lock-in ends, you can reprice with the same bank or refinance to another bank.

That makes the choice lopsided. Starting with HDB keeps both doors open: if bank rates stay well below 2.6%, you can refinance later. Starting with a bank closes the HDB door for that flat. Leaving a bank during its lock-in costs a penalty, which CPF Board says could be around 1.5% of the outstanding loan, or about $6,750 on $450,000.

When an HDB loan may fit better

  • You have little cash. The HDB loan needs no cash downpayment; a bank loan needs at least 5% of the price.
  • Your income is close to the limit. HDB's 3% test lets you borrow more than a bank's 4% test on the same income.
  • You may sell or repay early. There is no lock-in and no early repayment penalty.
  • You want to keep the choice open. You can move to a bank later when the rate suits you, but not back.
  • You want a rate that is not tied to market rates. The HDB rate follows the CPF OA rate, which has a legislated minimum of 2.5%, not SORA.

When a bank loan may fit better

  • You do not qualify for an HDB loan, for example because household income is above $16,000 for a family or no applicant is a Singapore Citizen.
  • You have the 5% cash and pass the 4% test with room to spare.
  • You will keep the loan past the lock-in and want the lower rate now. DBS's published package is below HDB's rate at every stage.
  • You accept SORA risk. A SORA package costs more than HDB if SORA climbs back above about 1.6% to 1.9%.
  • You need a longer tenure than HDB allows and accept that a bank loan over 25 years on an HDB flat is capped at 55% with 10% cash.

Compare the total package, not only year-one interest

Ask each bank for the fixed-period rate, subsequent pricing formula, lock-in period, partial-prepayment terms, full-redemption charge, repricing fee and estimated effective cost. Model a higher future rate before choosing a floating or short fixed package.

Use the bank’s property loan fact sheet to compare offers on the same loan amount and tenure. Check these three points before deciding:

  1. Monthly budget: compare the initial instalment and the bank’s illustration of payments if rates rise.
  2. After the initial rate: record the next pricing formula and reset frequency. Ask for the fixed-rate end date and lock-in end date separately.
  3. Your likely exit: if you sell, repay early or refinance, ask for the charges and any subsidy clawback in writing.

Start with your HFE outcome and the HDB loan calculator. Then compare the same property price, loan amount and tenure across offers.

HDB mortgage loan: which loans an HDB flat can take

Both options are mortgage loans secured on the flat. With an HDB housing loan the flat is mortgaged to HDB at the concessionary rate. With a bank loan it is mortgaged to the bank at the package rate. A flat carries one or the other, never both, and a flat already financed by a bank cannot move back to an HDB housing loan. An executive condominium bought from the developer and any private property cannot take an HDB housing loan at all, so for those the bank route is the only route.

Bank loan for an HDB flat: what the bank will ask for

  • For a resale flat at 75% LTV, at least 5% of the lower of purchase price and valuation in cash, with the remaining 20% in cash or CPF Ordinary Account savings. Cash-over-valuation is additional; lower LTV limits require a larger downpayment.
  • Income documents to assess the 30% MSR and 55% TDSR at the stress-test rate, counting existing car, renovation and personal loans.

The steps, in order:

  1. Get your HFE letter. It confirms you can buy the flat and whether you qualify for an HDB loan. Read the HFE letter guide for timing.
  2. Get an In-Principle Approval. The HDB Flat Portal's integrated loan application lets you request one from DBS, Hong Leong Finance, Maybank, OCBC and UOB while you apply for the HFE letter. You can also go to a bank directly.
  3. Get a Letter of Offer before you commit. HDB requires a valid Letter of Offer before you sign the Agreement for Lease for a new flat or exercise the Option to Purchase for a resale flat.
  4. Check the valuation. The loan is capped at 75% of the lower of price and valuation, so any cash-over-valuation is paid in cash on top of the downpayment.

What changed in 2024 to 2026

  • 20 August 2024: HDB cut its loan limit from 80% to 75% for complete resale applications from that date, and for new flats from the October 2024 sales exercise.
  • SORA fell: from 3.7012% in January 2024 to 1.0356% in May 2026, the lowest of the dates in the table above. It was 1.2748% on 29 Oct 2026.
  • Bank rates went below HDB's: the packages above start under 2.6%. CPF Board notes some bank rates went above 4% in 2022 and 2023, when HDB was the cheaper loan.
  • 24 August 2026: HDB loan income ceilings rose to $16,000 for families, $24,000 for extended families and $8,000 for singles, for HFE applications from that date. See the income-ceiling changes.
  • Unchanged: MAS's 4% stress-test floor and HDB's 3% floor, both in place since 30 September 2022, and the 2.5% legislated minimum for the CPF OA rate.

Frequently asked questions

What is the HDB housing loan interest rate in 2026?

HDB publishes a 2.6% concessionary rate for 1 October to 31 December 2026. The rate is pegged 0.1 percentage point above the prevailing CPF Ordinary Account rate and can be reviewed quarterly.

Is a bank loan cheaper than an HDB loan now?

At the rates banks published on 30 October 2026, yes for the first years. DBS advertises 1.78% for years one to three on a package pegged below the CPF OA rate, and UOB advertises 3-month compounded SORA plus 0.70%, about 1.97% at the 29 October 2026 SORA of 1.2748%, against 2.6% for HDB. A bank loan needs at least 5% cash, is tested at 4% instead of 3%, usually has a lock-in, and cannot be switched back to an HDB loan.

What is the bank loan interest rate for an HDB flat?

There is no single rate. On 30 October 2026, DBS published 1.78% for years one to three, 2.20% for years four and five and 2.40% after that. UOB published 3-month compounded SORA plus 0.70% in years one and two, plus 0.80% in year three and plus 1.00% after that. These are advertised rates; only a Letter of Offer confirms yours.

What is the stress test rate for an HDB bank loan?

Banks must work out your instalment at the higher of 4% or the thereafter rate of your package, under MAS rules in place since 30 September 2022. HDB uses the higher of 3% or its own loan rate. On a $450,000 loan over 25 years, that means about $7,918 of monthly income for a bank loan against about $7,114 for an HDB loan under the 30% MSR.

What is the maximum bank loan tenure for an HDB flat?

MAS allows up to 30 years. But for an HDB flat, a tenure over 25 years, or a loan that runs past age 65, cuts the bank loan limit from 75% to 55% and raises the minimum cash downpayment from 5% to 10%. An HDB loan is capped at 25 years.

Can I switch from a bank loan back to an HDB loan?

No. CPF Board states that a flat already financed with a bank loan cannot switch back to an HDB housing loan for that property.

How much cash is required for an HDB flat with a bank loan?

For a resale flat at 75% LTV, at least 5% of the lower of price and valuation must be cash. The remaining 20% can be cash or CPF OA, subject to the applicable rules. Cash-over-valuation and any funding shortfall require additional cash.

Does the 30% MSR apply to a bank loan for an HDB flat?

Yes. The Mortgage Servicing Ratio applies to housing loans for HDB flats and executive condominiums whether the lender is HDB or a bank, and a bank loan must also pass the 55% Total Debt Servicing Ratio at the stress-test rate.
