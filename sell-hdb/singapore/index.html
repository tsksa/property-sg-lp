How an HDB sale works

From first call to the Resale Completion Appointment.

Most HDB sales run 3–4 months end to end: 4–8 weeks to find the right buyer, then roughly 8 weeks of HDB process after the resale application is accepted. Every stage has a clear deliverable — you always know where you stand.

01

Free consult & valuation

30 minutes, no obligation. I confirm your MOP date, walk you through comparables and an indicative valuation, and give you a realistic price range before you commit to anything.

02

Intent to Sell & listing launch

We register your Intent to Sell on the HDB Resale Portal at least 7 days before any Option is granted. Professional photos, staging advice, listing copy — live across 4 portals and my private network within 48 hours of sign-off.

03

Viewings, offers & the Option to Purchase

Only buyers holding a valid HFE letter view your flat. I handle the negotiation and the OTP; the buyer then has a 21-day option period to exercise it.

04

Resale application to completion

Both sides submit the resale application within 7 days of each other. From HDB's acceptance, completion is about 8 weeks — I coordinate the endorsements and the Resale Completion Appointment. You show up when needed.

Before you ask

The questions HDB sellers ask most.

"Is 2% really the right commission?"

2% is the Singapore industry standard for sales, and what I charge. Cheaper agents typically cut corners on photography, listing placement, or follow-up — and the savings vanish when you close $10–30k below what your flat could have fetched.

"My flat hasn't reached its MOP yet."

You can't sell before the Minimum Occupation Period — but you can plan. Sellers who have pricing, presentation and their next-home plan ready 3–6 months before MOP consistently outperform those who rush. Free consult, no commitment.

"How long does it usually take?"

Listing to Option: 4–8 weeks typical. Resale application to completion: about 8 weeks of HDB process once accepted. End to end: 3–4 months. I'll give you a specific range for your flat and town at the free consult.

"How do I know you're not just another pushy agent?"

I'm selective and turn down bad-fit listings. Verify my CEA registration (R009618D) on the CEA public register in 30 seconds. Every number is on record before you sign anything.
