What is the median HDB resale price in Bukit Panjang?

The median HDB resale price in Bukit Panjang is $610,000, based on 780 registered transactions in the 12 months to 2026-09.

Which streets in Bukit Panjang have the most HDB resale sales?

In the 12 months to 2026-09, the streets in Bukit Panjang with the most HDB resale transactions were Segar Rd (167 sales), Senja Rd (153 sales) and Jelapang Rd (81 sales), out of 780 sales in the town.

Have HDB prices in Bukit Panjang gone up or down over the past year?

Like for like, HDB resale prices in Bukit Panjang have fallen 0.5% over the past year. This compares the median price per square foot of the same flat types and lease ages in the 12 months to 2026-09 against the prior 12 months, so a year when more newer flats sold does not count as a price rise.
