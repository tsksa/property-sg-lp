Common questions

How many HDB towns does this cover?

26 towns across Singapore, each with a 12-month resale median computed from official transactions.

Where does this HDB resale price data come from?

The Housing & Development Board's official HDB Resale Flat Prices dataset, published via data.gov.sg under the Singapore Open Data Licence v1.0.

What is the median HDB resale price in Singapore?

$630,000, across 24,652 registered HDB resale transactions in the 12 months to 2026-09. By flat type: 3-room $440,000, 4-room $630,000, 5-room $740,000 and Executive $915,000.

Are HDB resale prices going down in 2026?

No. Like for like, HDB resale prices across Singapore have risen 0.6% in the 12 months to 2026-09 compared with the 12 months before. This compares the median price per square foot of the same flat types and lease ages in each town, and describes past sales, not a forecast.

Which HDB town has the highest median resale price?

Bukit Timah, with a 12-month median of $1,010,000.

Which town has the cheapest 3-room HDB resale flats?

Toa Payoh, with a median 3-room resale price of $380,000 across 420 sales in the 12 months to 2026-09. Next was Geylang at $382,500. Only towns with at least 10 3-room sales are compared.

Which town has the cheapest 4-room HDB resale flats?

Jurong West, with a median 4-room resale price of $535,000 across 590 sales in the 12 months to 2026-09. Next was Jurong East at $548,000. Only towns with at least 10 4-room sales are compared.

Which town has the cheapest 5-room HDB resale flats?

Jurong West, with a median 5-room resale price of $635,000 across 431 sales in the 12 months to 2026-09. Next was Sembawang at $640,000. Only towns with at least 10 5-room sales are compared.

Which town has the cheapest Executive HDB resale flats?

Sembawang, with a median Executive resale price of $751,000 across 57 sales in the 12 months to 2026-09. Next was Jurong West at $781,900. Only towns with at least 10 Executive sales are compared.
