Common questions

What is the median HDB resale price in Jurong West?

The median HDB resale price in Jurong West is $565,000, based on 1474 registered transactions in the 12 months to 2026-08.

What is the median price per square foot (psf) for HDB flats in Jurong West?

The median price is $518 psf across all flat types, based on transactions in the 12 months to 2026-08.

Have HDB prices in Jurong West gone up or down over the past year?

Median resale prices in Jurong West have risen 0.9% over the past year, comparing the 12 months to 2026-08 against the prior 12-month period.
