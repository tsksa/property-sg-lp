Common questions

What is the median HDB resale price in Central Area?

The median HDB resale price in Central Area is $756,000, based on 174 registered transactions in the 12 months to 2026-09.

Which streets in Central Area have the most HDB resale sales?

In the 12 months to 2026-09, the streets in Central Area with the most HDB resale transactions were Cantonment Rd (52 sales), Tg Pagar Plaza (18 sales) and Veerasamy Rd (18 sales), out of 174 sales in the town.

Have HDB prices in Central Area gone up or down over the past year?

Like for like, HDB resale prices in Central Area have fallen 0.2% over the past year. This compares the median price per square foot of the same flat types and lease ages in the 12 months to 2026-09 against the prior 12 months, so a year when more newer flats sold does not count as a price rise.

What was the highest HDB resale price in Central Area in the last 12 months?

$1,720,000, for a 5 Room flat at Blk 1C Cantonment Rd, storeys 19 to 21, sold in 2026-09. It is the highest of 174 registered resale transactions in Central Area in the 12 months to 2026-09.

In the 12 months to 2026-09, 3-room resale flats in Central Area sold for a median $550,000 across 65 sales. Half sold between $500,000 and $578,000.
