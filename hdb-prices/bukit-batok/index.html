Common questions

What is the median HDB resale price in Bukit Batok?

The median HDB resale price in Bukit Batok is $610,000, based on 1386 registered transactions in the 12 months to 2026-08.

What is the median price per square foot (psf) for HDB flats in Bukit Batok?

The median price is $618 psf across all flat types, based on transactions in the 12 months to 2026-08.

Have HDB prices in Bukit Batok gone up or down over the past year?

Median resale prices in Bukit Batok have risen 3.4% over the past year, comparing the 12 months to 2026-08 against the prior 12-month period.
