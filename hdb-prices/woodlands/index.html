Common questions

What is the median HDB resale price in Woodlands?

The median HDB resale price in Woodlands is $585,000, based on 1826 registered transactions in the 12 months to 2026-09.

Which streets in Woodlands have the most HDB resale sales?

In the 12 months to 2026-09, the streets in Woodlands with the most HDB resale transactions were Woodlands St 13 (141 sales), Woodlands Dr 50 (134 sales) and Woodlands Ring Rd (121 sales), out of 1826 sales in the town.

Have HDB prices in Woodlands gone up or down over the past year?

Like for like, HDB resale prices in Woodlands have fallen 0.3% over the past year. This compares the median price per square foot of the same flat types and lease ages in the 12 months to 2026-09 against the prior 12 months, so a year when more newer flats sold does not count as a price rise.

What was the highest HDB resale price in Woodlands in the last 12 months?

$1,270,000, for an Executive flat at Blk 850 Woodlands St 82, storeys 10 to 12, sold in 2025-10. It is the highest of 1826 registered resale transactions in Woodlands in the 12 months to 2026-09.
