Common questions

What is the median HDB resale price in Bukit Merah?

The median HDB resale price in Bukit Merah is $790,000, based on 908 registered transactions in the 12 months to 2026-09.

Which streets in Bukit Merah have the most HDB resale sales?

In the 12 months to 2026-09, the streets in Bukit Merah with the most HDB resale transactions were Telok Blangah St 31 (76 sales), Jln Membina (62 sales) and Telok Blangah Cres (62 sales), out of 908 sales in the town.

Have HDB prices in Bukit Merah gone up or down over the past year?

Like for like, HDB resale prices in Bukit Merah have risen 1.7% over the past year. This compares the median price per square foot of the same flat types and lease ages in the 12 months to 2026-09 against the prior 12 months, so a year when more newer flats sold does not count as a price rise.
