Common questions

What is the median HDB resale price in Queenstown?

The median HDB resale price in Queenstown is $850,000, based on 751 registered transactions in the 12 months to 2026-09.

Which streets in Queenstown have the most HDB resale sales?

In the 12 months to 2026-09, the streets in Queenstown with the most HDB resale transactions were Dawson Rd (207 sales), Ghim Moh Link (77 sales) and Strathmore Ave (65 sales), out of 751 sales in the town.

Have HDB prices in Queenstown gone up or down over the past year?

Like for like, HDB resale prices in Queenstown have risen 5.6% over the past year. This compares the median price per square foot of the same flat types and lease ages in the 12 months to 2026-09 against the prior 12 months, so a year when more newer flats sold does not count as a price rise.

What was the highest HDB resale price in Queenstown in the last 12 months?

$1,700,000, for a 5 Room flat at Blk 92 Dawson Rd, storeys 19 to 21, sold in 2026-02. It is the highest of 751 registered resale transactions in Queenstown in the 12 months to 2026-09.
