Common questions

What is the median HDB resale price in Serangoon?

The median HDB resale price in Serangoon is $670,000, based on 387 registered transactions in the 12 months to 2026-09.

Which streets in Serangoon have the most HDB resale sales?

In the 12 months to 2026-09, the streets in Serangoon with the most HDB resale transactions were Serangoon Nth Ave 4 (72 sales), Serangoon Nth Ave 1 (52 sales) and Serangoon Ave 2 (49 sales), out of 387 sales in the town.

Have HDB prices in Serangoon gone up or down over the past year?

Like for like, HDB resale prices in Serangoon have risen 2.7% over the past year. This compares the median price per square foot of the same flat types and lease ages in the 12 months to 2026-09 against the prior 12 months, so a year when more newer flats sold does not count as a price rise.
