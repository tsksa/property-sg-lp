What is the median HDB resale price in Pasir Ris?

The median HDB resale price in Pasir Ris is $722,388, based on 766 registered transactions in the 12 months to 2026-09.

Which streets in Pasir Ris have the most HDB resale sales?

In the 12 months to 2026-09, the streets in Pasir Ris with the most HDB resale transactions were Pasir Ris St 51 (116 sales), Pasir Ris St 71 (96 sales) and Pasir Ris St 21 (84 sales), out of 766 sales in the town.

Have HDB prices in Pasir Ris gone up or down over the past year?

Like for like, HDB resale prices in Pasir Ris have risen 1.2% over the past year. This compares the median price per square foot of the same flat types and lease ages in the 12 months to 2026-09 against the prior 12 months, so a year when more newer flats sold does not count as a price rise.
