Joe’s Take: Sembawang Road EC

This project is best suited to eligible EC households who want a smaller north-region development and prioritise owner occupation over short-term resale flexibility. The verified record places Sembawang Road EC at Sembawang Road in D27, with 265 homes on a 99-year leasehold tenure by Oriental Pacific Development. That makes buyer fit more important than a generic “best launch” label.

The strongest advantage is the EC format and limited 265-home scale, which may appeal to buyers who prefer a more compact community than the typical mass-market launch.
