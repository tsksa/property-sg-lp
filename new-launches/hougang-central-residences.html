Launch date and timeline · checked 13 Sept 2026

Hougang Central Residences launch date: what is confirmed, what is not.

  1. 14 Jan 2026

    Site awarded to the CapitaLand and UOL consortium

    CapitaLand Integrated Commercial Trust, CapitaLand Development and UOL Group were awarded the Hougang Central site, tendered in December 2025, at about S$1.5 billion or S$1,179 per square foot per plot ratio. CICT develops and keeps the retail; CapitaLand Development and UOL build the homes for sale.

  2. 12 Aug 2026

    Launch guidance in UOL's half-year results

    UOL's 1H 2026 results presentation lists the Hougang Central residential component, over 800 homes, with a target launch in the second half of 2027.

  3. 13 Sept 2026

    Sales status checked

    UOL still lists the project as “Site at Hougang Central”: no project name, unit mix, floor plans, preview date, price list or balance-unit count has been published. “Hougang Central Residences” is the working name used in the market.

  4. Q4 2027

    Planned launch in the second half of 2027

    UOL's guidance is a second-half-2027 launch, so the sale is expected by the fourth quarter of 2027. The quarter has not been narrowed and no preview date has been announced.

  5. Not announced

    Project name, preview, booking and completion dates

    Not announced. CapitaLand guides completion around 2030 to 2031, when the Cross Island Line interchange at Hougang is also due. This timeline is updated when the developers publish details, and Joe can confirm the first official release by WhatsApp.

Confirmed steps come from the developer's SGX filings. Expected means the developer has stated a plan, not a date. Nothing here is estimated by this site.
