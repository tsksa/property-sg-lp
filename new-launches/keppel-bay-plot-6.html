Keppel Bay Plot 6 launch, units and project status

The project is still pre-launch. Keppel's latest annual-report property schedule and the current corporate launch pipeline provide the clearest verified picture; final sale materials remain pending.

What is Keppel Bay Plot 6?

Keppel Bay Plot 6 is a proposed 99-year leasehold waterfront condominium at Keppel Bay. Keppel's 2025 annual report lists Keppel Bay Pte Ltd as the holder and a land area of 43,701 square metres.

How many units will Keppel Bay Plot 6 have?

Keppel's latest annual-report property schedule proposes 84 units. An older PropNex launch pipeline lists 86, so this page uses the newer developer filing and will update again when official sale materials confirm the final count.

When will Keppel Bay Plot 6 launch?

The PropNex FY2025 launch pipeline targets Q4 2026. Keppel has not published an exact preview or booking date in the official sources checked as of 25 August 2026.

Are the Keppel Bay Plot 6 price list and floor plans available?

No verified developer price list, floor plan, showflat date or booking date was available in the official sources checked as of 25 August 2026. Treat third-party prices and appointment dates as provisional.

Is Oceania at Keppel Bay the official project name?

Not in the official sources checked as of 25 August 2026. Keppel's latest annual-report property schedule still uses Keppel Bay Plot 6, so Oceania at Keppel Bay should be treated as an unconfirmed marketing name.

Joe’s Take: Keppel Bay Plot 6

This project is best suited to waterfront-focused buyers comfortable evaluating a small, premium Keppel Bay project before its final identity, programme and launch timing are announced. The verified record places Keppel Bay Plot 6 at Keppel Island in D04, with 84 homes on a 99-year leasehold tenure by Keppel Land. That makes buyer fit more important than a generic “best launch” label.

Its strongest advantage is scarcity: Keppel's latest annual-report property schedule proposes only 84 homes in a Keppel Bay waterfront location, creating a differentiated proposition. The current verified launch line is: Provisional launch window: Q4 2026 · no balance units yet. This is a timing statement, not a price or availability claim; final stacks, floors and layouts have not been released.

The main risk is substantial uncertainty. Q4 2026 is a pipeline target rather than an announced preview date, no verified pricing is available, and a boutique waterfront project may carry a high premium with limited comparable evidence. Before choosing, compare three things once they are released: the official floor plans, the total entry quantum and the most credible active alternatives. I would only shortlist the project when those checks support the buyer’s own timeline and exit plan—not because an early launch headline creates urgency.
