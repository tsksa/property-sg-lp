Subject: HDB ceiling now $16,000; qualifying new ECs $18,000 — what changes for you?

Preview: The income limits changed, but your HFE date and the EC's land tender still matter.

Hi,

If your household narrowly exceeded the previous HDB income ceiling, it is worth reviewing your housing options again.

For HFE applications from 24 August 2026, the general family ceiling for subsidised HDB housing and HDB loans rises from $14,000 to $16,000. Eligible singles buying alone move from $7,000 to $8,000.

The $18,000 figure is different: it applies to new EC units on sites whose land sale tender closes on or after 24 August 2026. It does not automatically apply to existing EC launches.

Three things to check before changing your plans:

- Your existing HFE: already-eligible holders need no action solely for this change. Some previously income-ineligible applicants may reapply, but a fresh application changes the income-assessment period. Check your case before cancelling anything.
- Your budget: eligibility is not loan approval. The higher ceiling does not automatically increase EHG entitlement or change repayment rates.
- Your dates: the next BTO exercise is in November 2026. HDB advises completing HFE documents by 25 September. The extra ballot chances for eligible families with children start only in February 2027.
